## Nov 19, 2026: first public release

The source is now open, under the MIT licence. What the site does at this point:

- **Home** opens with a masthead and Continue, led by the title you're furthest behind on, then picks for you, trending, popular this season and upcoming. +1 logs an episode or chapter from anywhere, and reaching the total completes the title with an Undo.
- **Title pages** with a duotone banner tinted by the cover, the score, relations, characters and staff, recommendations, tags and a Stats tab. The list editor opens from any title: status, progress, score, dates, notes, custom lists and privacy.
- **My List**, tabs by status, as a table or a grid, edited in place.
- **Seasonal and Schedule:** the season chart, and the week's airings in your own time zone. In manga mode, new releases.
- **For You:** picks from the titles you rated highest, measured against your own scoring, each saying why. Favourite studios count, the other list suggests its manga or anime, and Tune picks leaves out what you don't want.
- **Search and browse** with AniList's filters, kept in the address; a search also finds characters, staff, studios and users.
- **People's pages** (yours included) with their lists and stats, plus staff, character and studio pages.
- **Settings** saved to AniList, notifications, and an anime/manga switch for the whole site.
- **The look:** editorial, drawn from anime and manga print. A warm off-black page, cream ink, Fraunces headings, ruled sections instead of cards, and each title's cover colour tinting its page. Motion is quick and quiet, and only fades remain under reduced motion.
