# Code of conduct

miruyo is a small project built for people who love anime and manga. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their background, identity or experience, and everyone deserves to be treated with respect.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Assume good intent, and say what you'd change and why.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and give it the way you'd want to receive it.
- Keep discussions on topic and spoiler-free unless a thread is clearly about the story.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a shared space.

## Where this applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour bothers you, contact the maintainer privately through the contact details on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/jasanpreetn9). Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer will review each report and may respond with a warning, by removing comments or contributions, or with a temporary or permanent ban from the project, depending on how serious it is.

This code of conduct is adapted from the ideas in the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
