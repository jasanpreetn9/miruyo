Home

Continue, ready first. Under the hero, the title with the most new episodes gets a featured card and the big +1; the rest queue beside it (new, then airing soonest, Paused last). Undo lives in the toast. Caught up becomes a countdown (1i).

Hero = banner + cover. The banner bleeds edge-to-edge and fades left and down into the page; the cover sits on top with its own glow. Missing banners fall back to the cover, blurred and enlarged.

Rhythm. Posters rows alternate with one wide-card row (Upcoming) so long scrolls don't feel like a spreadsheet. The mode toggle in the top bar is live — try Manga.
