Search & browse

Filters stay visible on desktop in a left rail; results update live (300ms debounce) — no “Apply” button. On mobile, a bottom sheet with Apply, because each change would otherwise reflow under your thumb.

Chips mirror state. Every active filter appears as a removable chip above results, with the count. The rail and chips are the same state, so either can undo. A Home row's “See all” opens Browse with its filters set and names the list in the heading (“Trending now”, “Popular this season”) instead of “Browse anime”.

Mode-aware fields. Anime shows Season/Year; Manga swaps in Country of origin and Publishing status. Toggle mode or the view (grid/compact) on 2a.
