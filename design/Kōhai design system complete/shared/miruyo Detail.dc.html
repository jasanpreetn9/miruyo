Title detail

The title's colour owns the page. Banner, cover glow, progress fill and the countdown accent all derive from the cover colour, so every detail page feels like its show while the chrome stays constant.

Your status sits under the cover — the list button, progress stepper and score, then the next episode's countdown and the facts, all in one sticky column so updating never requires scrolling past the synopsis.

Anime vs manga. Switch the top-bar toggle on 3a: the countdown and Trailer button become chapters + volumes and authors with roles (AniList has no publisher or release cadence). Relations come before Characters, which show as many as fit in a row. “Remind me” waits on reminders (open decision 3).
