Seasonal & schedule

One nav item, two views (after AniChart). The season chart (6a) is everything premiering, in sections: TV, TV Short, Leftovers (still airing from earlier seasons), Movie, OVA / ONA / Special. The header picks a season or Airing, the week's schedule (6c).

Cards say when, not just what. The cover fills the card's height, with the title and studio beside it; three to a row on desktop. “Ep 2 of 12 airing in · 18 hours, 31 mins”, the source or sequel, three lines of synopsis, genres in the cover colour, AniList score with a face, and this season's popularity rank. + is the status menu; titles on your list get a cover-colour ring.

The week reads top to bottom. Today first, then the rest of the week, days already past at the end under “Earlier this week”. Your shows glow, carry progress and a +1 once out. Manga has no schedule on AniList, so its slot is New releases: your series still releasing, then newly serialized.
