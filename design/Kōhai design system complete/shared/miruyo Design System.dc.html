DESIGN SYSTEM · v0.1

The artwork is the interface. Everything else gets out of the way.

Covers lead
Neutral, cool surfaces so cover colour carries the page. Chrome never competes with art.
One tap to +1
Progress is a first-class control on every card, row and page — never behind a modal.
One product, two modes
Anime = Beni (coral). Manga = Fuji (lilac). Only the mode accent changes; structure doesn't.
Dense, not cluttered
4px grid, strict type scale, metadata in a quieter tier than titles.

04 — ICONS · MOTION

Iconography & motion

Material Symbols Rounded, weight 400, outlined at rest and filled when active. 20px in UI, 24px in tab bars. Motion is quick and quiet — it confirms, it doesn't perform.

05 — ACTIONS

Buttons

Accent buttons use dark ink (#1A0B08, 6.1:1) — white on coral fails AA. Heights: 32 / 40 / 48; on touch every target is ≥44px even when the visual is smaller. Focus is always a 2px light ring with 2px offset.

09 — FEEDBACK

Modals, toasts, skeletons

Every list change is optimistic and confirmed with a toast carrying Undo for 5s. Destructive actions get a modal. Loading uses skeletons shaped like the real layout — no spinners on content.
