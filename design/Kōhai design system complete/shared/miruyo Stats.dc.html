Profile & settings

One accent per chart. Charts use the mode accent for the primary series and neutral greys for the rest; status colours appear only where status is the data. Every bar is labelled — no legend hunting.

Readable on dark. Gridlines at 6% white, values in Geist Mono at text-mid, hover lifts the bar to text-high and shows an exact tooltip.

Anime / Manga tabs swap units (episodes → chapters, days watched → volumes) and the accent. Everything is computed from your list, so the monthly chart counts titles finished (AniList keeps no per-episode history). The profile (/profile, the Profile tab) leads with the AniList banner and avatar, then favourites on the left and Stats on the right; favourite titles follow the Anime / Manga switch. The gear opens Settings (7c).
