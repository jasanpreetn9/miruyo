Login & onboarding

One button. AniList OAuth is the only sign-in, so the page is one decision plus a plain-language note on what we can and can't see.

Three settings, then go. Default mode, title language (Romaji vs English matters for long titles) and adult content. Title language and adult content are saved to your AniList account; there's no “Both” mode. Everything else lives in Settings.

Empty list? We skip straight to the quick-pick grid from Home (see Home 1d) instead of dropping people on an empty page.
