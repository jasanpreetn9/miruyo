Moving between places

Mode lives on the toggle. Drag the toggle's thumb, or tap it, to switch between Anime and Manga. Swiping content never changes mode, because switching mode by accident would be the most disorienting mistake in the app.

Content swipes page. My List status pages swipe sideways, and the tab underline follows your finger. Schedule is different: days stack vertically, and swiping the week bar changes the week (see Schedule gestures). Swipes that start in the 20px nearest the left edge are left for the system back gesture.

Hold a poster to peek. Wherever a poster card appears (Browse, For You, My List grid), holding it opens a peek with quick actions. Try both on 13a. Row gestures are covered on Rows.
