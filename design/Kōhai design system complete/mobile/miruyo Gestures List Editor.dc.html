List editor gestures

Nothing to save. Every change goes to AniList as you make it, so pulling the sheet down just closes it. Only a half-typed note asks before closing. The sheet is the mobile form of List editor 4b.

Drag the numbers. Scrub the episode count sideways to jump several episodes, or hold +1 to count up. The score cells work like a slider: drag across them, and the left half of a cell gives the half point.

Hold to delete. Delete fills while you hold it and only removes the entry when full. An Undo stays up for 5 seconds afterwards. Try all of it on 20a.
