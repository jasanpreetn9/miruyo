SCREEN 11 · GESTURES · ROWS

Row gestures

One row, four gestures. The same row is used in Home › Continue and My List. Swipe right to +1. A short swipe left is −1, and a long one opens More. Hold to open quick actions. Pull down at the top of the list to sync with AniList.

Axis lock at 8px. Vertical movement scrolls; horizontal movement belongs to the row. Mode never switches by swiping content, only on the top-bar toggle (see Navigation).

Taught once. On first visit the top row demonstrates the gestures and a coach mark explains them. "Got it" saves that it's been seen. Buttons stay as fallbacks, so gestures are never the only way in. Try it on 11a.
