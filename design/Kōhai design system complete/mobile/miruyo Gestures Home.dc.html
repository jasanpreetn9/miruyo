Home gestures

The hero is a carousel. Five airing titles, 6s each. The active dot fills with the cover colour as a timer. Swipe to page, tap a dot to jump, hold to pause. Touching it pauses autoplay, and so does scrolling it out of view.

The featured card is a row. Continue's featured card uses the row grammar: right +1, short left −1, long left More. When it's caught up, the card hands off to the next ready title.

No traps. Drags never wrap at the ends; they rubber-band. Only autoplay loops. The Up next rail scrolls natively, and holding a poster opens the peek from Navigation. Try it on 14a.
