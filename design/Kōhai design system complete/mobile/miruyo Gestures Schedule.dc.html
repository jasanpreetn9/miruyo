Schedule gestures

Days stack, the strip scrubs. The schedule reads top to bottom, as in Seasonal. A pinned day strip follows your scroll. Tap a day to jump to it, or drag along the strip to scrub through the week.

Weeks page on the header. Swipe the week bar to go to the previous or next week, up to four weeks either way. Content swipes stay with the slots, so a vertical list never pages sideways.

Slots swipe by what they can do. Swipe right on an aired episode to log it (+1), on an upcoming one to set a reminder, and on someone else's show to add it to Planning. Left is −1. Try it on 16a.
