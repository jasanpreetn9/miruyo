Onboarding gestures

Only Continue moves forward. Steps never swipe ahead, so a stray swipe can't skip a choice. The edge swipe goes back a step. It never goes back past sign-in: once you're signed in, signing out lives in Settings.

Pull down to back out. AniList's approval page opens in a sheet. Pulling it down cancels the sign-in, says plainly that nothing was shared, and returns you to the login screen.

Paint to pick. On the empty-list quick-pick from Home 1d, drag across posters to pick several at once. The first poster you touch decides whether the drag adds or removes. Try it on 19a.
