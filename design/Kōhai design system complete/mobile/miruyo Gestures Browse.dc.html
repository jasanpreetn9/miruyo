Browse gestures

Search gets out of the way. Scrolling down slides the search bar away and keeps the filter chips pinned. Any scroll up brings it back.

Pinch sets density. Pinch in to go from 2 across to 3 across, then to the compact list. Spread to go back. In the list, rows follow the row grammar: right adds to Planning, left hides the title.

The sheet is a draft. Score thumbs tick in 0.5 steps and the Apply count updates as you drag. Dragging the sheet down discards the draft, and the toast offers Restore. Try it on 15a.
