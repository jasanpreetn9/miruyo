Dismiss and drag

Detail is a card. When Detail is scrolled to the top, dragging down anywhere shrinks it and Home shows through behind. Releasing past 140px, or flicking, closes it. The chevron does the same with a tap.

Edits save as you go. Every change in Edit entry goes to AniList straight away, so pulling the sheet down just closes it, the same as the List editor. Only a half-typed note asks first.

Score is ten cells. Drag across them and the score follows your finger; the left half of a cell gives the half point. Tap a cell, or tap it again for .5. Try it on 12a.
