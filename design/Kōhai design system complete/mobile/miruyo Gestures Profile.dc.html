Profile gestures

Scrub any chart. Drag sideways across the score bars, the months or the activity grid. The exact value reads out in the card header, so your finger never covers it. After you let go it stays for 1.5s.

Sideways means scrub, up and down means scroll. A chart only starts scrubbing after 6px of sideways movement, so scrolling the page past a chart never highlights a bar.

Swipe the totals to switch. The four stat cards swipe between Anime and Manga, and the pinned toggle's thumb can be dragged. Both stay in sync with the top bar toggle. Try it on 18a.
