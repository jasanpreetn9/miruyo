For You gestures

Every pick is a yes or no. This is the only grid where posters swipe: right adds to Planning, left is Not this. For You has no pages to swipe between, so nothing competes for the gesture.

Hold to see why. Holding a pick opens the peek from Navigation with the reasoning on top: the title you rated, your score, the genres they share, and how many AniList users recommend it.

Seeds can be muted. Tap a seed to filter the picks. Swipe it up to stop using it, and its picks leave together. Every action has Undo. Try it on 17a.
