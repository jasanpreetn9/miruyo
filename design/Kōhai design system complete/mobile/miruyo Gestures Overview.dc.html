SCREEN 22 · GESTURES · OVERVIEW

Gesture overview

One grammar, eleven screens. Screens 11–21 each specify their own gestures. This page collects the rules they share, so a new screen can start from here instead of copying a neighbour.

Exceptions are on purpose. Where a screen breaks a shared rule, 22d says why. If a change can't give a reason as good as those, it should follow the rule.

Drift is resolved. Six values that differed between screens without a reason were brought in line on Oct 9, 2026. 22e records what changed and where.

22bHaptic scale
vibrationSelectionTicks while something changes under your finger: a page, a step, an item painted, a target entered.
vibrationLightA swipe arms, or a small change commits: −1, a switch flips, a reorder drops, Undo.
vibrationMediumSomething opens or lifts: a peek, quick actions, a carried stack.
vibrationSuccessA positive commit: +1, added to Planning, moved, synced, last episode reached.
vibrationWarningSomething was removed, or a close was blocked. Never used for a mistake the user can't see.
22cWho wins · the first match takes the touch
1Overlay openAn open sheet, peek or status panel captures every touch.
2Left edgeThe 20px strip goes to OS back (or the screen's own back).
3Owner zonesToggles, sliders, the day strip, charts, reorder handles and Edit circles own every drag that starts on them.
4Item swipesRows, slots, the featured card and For You picks take horizontal drags.
5PagerWhatever horizontal movement is left pages, where the screen has pages.
6ScrollVertical movement, and anything nothing else claimed.
